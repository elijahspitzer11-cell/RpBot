📜 Bot Commands

Memory

Clear - Clears the bots memory of the conversation in that channel.
/Clear or !Clear

Summary - Gives the summary of what is going on in that channel. What RP is happening and what the AI remembers.
/Summary or !Summary

Roleplay Commands

Start - You do not have to @ the bot
/Start or !start

Stop - You have to @ the bot
/stop or !stop

Bot Rules

Important - Sets the Roleplays rule for the bot. This will not be cleared when the bot wipes its brain
/important (for text only) or !Important (with an image)

Server Helper Commands

TicketSetup - Allows people to make tickets
!TicketSetup or /ticketsetup

I wouldn't use important update because it may get removed.
